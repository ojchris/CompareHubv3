FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first compare.

Is CompareHub free to use?

Yes, CompareHub is completely free for shoppers. You can search, filter, and compare any product with no fees, no account required.

Do I buy directly from CompareHub?

No — CompareHub is a price comparison platform. We show you prices from verified vendors and link you directly to their store to complete your purchase.

How do I know the prices are accurate?

Vendors update their prices directly on our platform. We also run automated checks to flag stale or suspicious listings. Prices are refreshed frequently throughout the day.

What cities are covered?

We currently cover Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, Ibadan, Enugu, Kaduna, and Benin City — with more cities launching soon.

Can I compare food and electronics together?

The Compare Cart is category-specific, so you'd compare electronics with electronics and food with food. This ensures meaningful comparisons on the right attributes.

How do I track my order after buying?

Since you buy directly from the vendor, tracking is handled by them. CompareHub provides a receipt and links you to the vendor's order tracking page.

Still have questions?

Our support team is available Mon-Sat, 8am-8pm (WAT).
