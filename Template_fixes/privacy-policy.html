1. Introduction

CompareHub Nigeria Limited ("CompareHub", "we", "us", or "our") operates the CompareHub website and mobile application (collectively, the "Platform"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your personal data. By using the CompareHub Platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information collected automatically, and information from third parties.

Information You Provide

Name, email address, phone number, city, and account credentials when you register. Contact form submissions, reviews, and ratings you submit on the Platform.

Information Collected Automatically

Device type, IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, search queries, compare cart activity, and session duration. Location data (city level) when you use location-based features, with your consent.

Information from Third Parties

If you sign in via Google or social login, we receive your name, email, and profile picture as permitted by that provider's privacy settings.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data only for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Providing, operating, and improving the CompareHub Platform and services.
  • Personalising your experience — showing relevant products and price alerts for your city.
  • Sending transactional emails, price drop notifications, and service updates (where you have opted in).
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and ensuring platform security.
  • Complying with Nigerian legal obligations and regulatory requirements.
  • Conducting aggregated analytics to understand usage patterns (no individual identification).

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Vendors: We may share limited contact information when you click through to a vendor's store or submit an enquiry to them directly.
  • With Service Providers: Trusted third-party partners (e.g. email delivery, analytics, cloud hosting) acting on our instructions under data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of CompareHub or its users.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to equivalent privacy protections.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy or as required by Nigerian law. Specifically:

  • Account data is retained for the duration of your account plus 6 months after account deletion.
  • Transaction logs and search history are retained for up to 24 months for fraud prevention purposes.
  • Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months.

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

6. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

Request a copy of your personal data we hold.

Right to Rectification

Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your data, subject to legal obligations.

Right to Object

Object to processing for direct marketing at any time.

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a machine-readable format.

Right to Restriction

Restrict processing while a dispute is resolved.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@comparehub.ng. We will respond within 30 days as required by law.

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and improve the Platform. Cookie types we use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the Platform to function — login sessions, compare cart state, and security tokens.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the Platform (page visits, search terms). We use anonymised data only.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your location, currency display, and UI preferences across sessions.

You can manage or disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect certain Platform features.

8. Third-Party Services

The CompareHub Platform contains links to vendor websites and third-party services. This Privacy Policy applies only to CompareHub's own operations. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of:

  • Vendor stores you visit after clicking "Buy" from CompareHub.
  • Payment processors or logistics partners involved in your transaction.
  • Social media platforms if you share content from CompareHub.

We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with through our Platform.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • TLS/HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between your device and our servers.
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive account credentials using bcrypt hashing.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments by certified professionals.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that may affect your rights, we will notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) as required by law.

10. Children's Privacy

The CompareHub Platform is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@comparehub.ng and we will promptly delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or Nigerian law. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Post a prominent notice on the Platform at least 14 days before the change takes effect.
  • Send a notification to registered users via email where the change materially affects their rights.

Continued use of the Platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent revision.
