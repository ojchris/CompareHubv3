CompareHub was born out of a simple frustration: why do the same products cost so differently across Nigerian vendors? We built the answer.

Our Story

In 2024, our founders Illerri and Christian were shopping for a MacBook in Lagos. They visited three stores on Computer Village — and discovered price differences of over ₦150,000 for the exact same laptop.

Within 6 months, the platform had expanded to food, groceries, and household goods — covering 8 Nigerian cities and onboarding over 180 verified vendors.

Today, CompareHub processes thousands of price comparisons every day, helping Nigerians save an average of 23% on every purchase.

They thought: "There has to be a better way." So they built one. CompareHub launched in 2025 as a simple price comparison tool for electronics in Lagos.

Our Mission

"To give every Nigerian shopper — from Lagos to all part of Nigeria — the information they need to always buy at the fairest price, from a vendor they can trust."

We believe price transparency is a right, not a privilege. CompareHub is free for all shoppers because saving money should never cost money.

What We Stand For
